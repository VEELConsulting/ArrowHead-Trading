Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

Last updated: May 2025

Who we are

This website is operated by ArrowHead Trading, an independent gold trading entity operating anonymously. For the purposes of this privacy policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to ArrowHead Trading. For any privacy-related enquiries, please use the contact page.

What information we collect

ArrowHead Trading does not directly collect, store, or process personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for the operation of this website.

Contact form submissions: When you submit a message via the contact form, your name, email address, and message content are transmitted to and stored by Formspree (our third-party form provider). We receive this information by email in order to respond to your enquiry. We do not store this data beyond what exists in our email inbox.

Technical data: When you visit this website, standard technical information may be logged by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit. This data is collected automatically and is not used by ArrowHead Trading for any marketing or tracking purpose.

Cookies

This website uses a single cookie stored in your browser's local storage to remember your preferred display theme (light or dark mode). This cookie contains no personal information and is not transmitted to any server. It exists solely to improve your experience on return visits.

We do not use any advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.

No tracking: ArrowHead Trading does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party tracking or analytics service. Your visits to this website are not profiled or monetised.

How we use your information

Information submitted via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We will not add you to any mailing list, share your details with third parties, or use your information for any purpose beyond the direct response to your message.

Third-party services

This website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

GitHub Pages — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical data from visitors as part of its standard infrastructure operation. Refer to GitHub's privacy policy at github.com/privacy for details.

Formspree — contact form submissions are processed by Formspree. Refer to Formspree's privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy for details.

TradingView — the performance page embeds a TradingView market data widget. TradingView may set cookies or collect data when you interact with this widget. Refer to TradingView's privacy policy at tradingview.com/policies for details.

Google Fonts — this website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Google may collect usage data in connection with this service. Refer to Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details.

Data retention

Contact form submissions received by email are retained only as long as necessary to manage the correspondence. We do not maintain a database of user data.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law (including the UK GDPR), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. As we hold minimal personal data — limited to contact form enquiries — requests can be made via the contact page and will be responded to within 30 days.

Children

This website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

External links

This website contains links to external websites including YouTube, TradingView, and prop firm platforms. Once you leave this website, this privacy policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of this website following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page to get in touch. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
