Q1 2025 — January to March
+31.4%74% win rate22 trades

The strongest quarter to date, driven by two high-conviction demand block entries in January and a trending sequence in March following FOMC commentary. The key learning was patience — three setups were identified that were not taken due to weak confluence, all of which would have been losers. The process held.

Max drawdown reached 4.2% in the second week of February following a fakeout at $2,940. Stop was honoured. Recovery came through disciplined re-engagement at the next key level. No revenge trading.
